# Sim Racing Morocco (SRM) Portal

Sim Racing Morocco is Morocco's sim racing community, racing Assetto Corsa
and Le Mans Ultimate together through Discord. This portal is the
community's public face — a place to see it's alive and competitive without
needing to be in the Discord.

**What's here:**

- **Overview** — a live snapshot of members, upcoming events, and recent results.
- **Standings** — season results across every completed event, ranked by
  wins, podiums, and consistency, not just points.
- **Events** — the upcoming and past race calendar.

It's read-only: nothing here is written by visitors, only shown. The data
comes from the community itself, synced from its Discord.
